The Order Blackreach is one of a number of the Witching Orders, this one in particular is quite disparate, their home-base is Greymount, a floating mountain turned on its head with a fortress city built on the flat and within the stone itself. The Greymount exerts its influence with its Knightly houses, which each occupy 1-2 large Ironwind ships, while all are different they generally have a central unit with a couple of large gun batteries on it along with the knights quarters and a monastery and then two large steel panels on either side attached via iron mesh which have rotating guns on them serving as tough defense. The Order Blackreach itself operates as a 'vassal' of Holy Calradia, reporting to Imperator Vormont II, Blessed of the Storm, Spring Bringer, Iron Hand.
