## Overview

A magical order of knights based in the Spire Mountains on the far south of the world, the continent of Arctis.

## Economy 

Some traders that have been vetted are allowed to enter into the front chamber of the order. These traders bring foods, news and interesting pieces of technology and in return they receive some of the rare materials extracted from the mountains, fully charged batteries, and occasionally other pieces of technology from the vaults in a secret bit of hypocrisy.

## Diplomacy

The main connection to the world is the Frost Rail, a train line which runs from a city on the southern tip of the eastern most continent to the monasteries and further into the southern ice. The train itself is capable of running across thousands miles of water before porting itself onto enormous rails which rocket across a thousand miles of ice.

## Operations

The knights errant are sent out into the world to explore and discover pieces of technology of interest. They join parties or journey alone. They carry various pieces of helpful technology with them including 'steam tags', 'blood extractors' and 'bolt guns'.

The southern ice has harsh winds which make air travel incredibly difficult even for specialised airships, A large launcher canon shoots a single man airship through the storm on a quest to pick up several of the steam tagged technologies and ferry them back to the monastery. The airships are disassembled at embassies and after some time the knight, the raw materials of the airship and their collected technology are ferried back to the monastery via the frost rail.
 
Knight errants are sent out in slightly upgraded in bastion airships, built for one person to live in for quite some time with better armour, faster engines and a larger living space. They do however have less storage and lack the reaching arm of the scouting bastions.
 
The sky-bastions themselves run on wood-oil fires, keeping the balloons afloat. The sky-bastions have furnaces also

## Notable Members

- **High MechanoMage Loren** While the order is very focussed on collecting and storing esoteric technology they are not very good innovators, mostly building on already found inventions. bit creepy but we like him, spends his time studying and storing technology and spends all his time studying the esoteric technologies brought to him from across the world. He is heavily augmented, notably he has replaced his lower half with a spiders lower quarters and an abdomen furnace, he also has no ears and several arms, some organic some mechanical. He also has a circle of servants who have also been heavily modified and augmented. Within his chambers there is a warforged colossus that acts as his body guard. He got the inspiration for his spider carapace from the ice barges of the order.

## Circles of the monastery
 
Circle of the furnace keepers Circle of the tech priests Circle of the knight errants Circle of the high witch
 
The monastery is nestled deep within a single great mountain, underneath the mountain is a great cavern in which vast and great mechanical legs are stored as a final contingency should the monastery ever fall. There is also a great cavern of fuel which powers a long range thruster. The great cavern of fuel contains a terrifying source of power which has been barely tapped so far.
